## Scope

The site's home page (`/`) and its model pages (`/models/<slug>/`), generated by `scripts/build.mjs` with `site/styles.css`. Visitor mode: **Experience**. The objects lead from the first viewport.

## Audience, job, constraints

- **Makers** find a model, check its size, triangle count and file, and download it once it is cleared.
- **The curious public** turn models over and share links.
- **Success** is "loved to explore".
- **Owner's anti-goals:** the site must not feel like a government office or a museum (stiff), and it must not feel cute or toy-like.
- **Navigation:** the home page is a place to wander. Objects are arranged where you'd meet them in Malaysia, and every model page leads to whatever is nearby, across categories.
- **Constraints:** every model is named as the real object it is, KL Rush appears only on the roadside billboards that advertise it (owner, 2026-11-09), and the site stays private and noindex.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The library is a drive through Malaysia. Places are exits off one road, and every object stands on the verge under its own brown attraction sign. It refuses the asset-store grid of equal thumbnail cards and the cream museum catalogue.

OWN-WORLD:
- **Palette:** expressway green boards, brown attraction signs and blue information boards in retroreflective white lettering, a red-ringed height-limit roundel, orange temporary-works signs, asphalt with white and yellow lane paint, pale concrete verges with black-and-white kerb stones, and white kilometre stones.
- **Type:** one road-sign grotesque, self-hosted.
- **Drawing:** SVG sign arrows and pictograms.

STORY: The visitor gets onto the road at the Skyline exit with Merdeka 118 turning. They read the exits on the gantry, scroll past exit after exit of real objects, and open one. Each model page names the object, shows its true size on signs, says plainly whether it can be downloaded, and points to what's nearby.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- **Top:** a full-width steel gantry. The library's route shield is on the left, then the current exit's lit board and the next two exits with arrows.
- **Below it:** the Skyline exit painted edge to edge in board green, with the exit name and its Malay name. On the left is a large live, auto-turning Merdeka 118 on the concrete verge, with its brown sign and a height-limit roundel (678.9 m). The other towers stand on the verge to the right, each under its own sign.
- **The primary action** is the objects themselves (each opens its page), plus the gantry's exit boards.

FORM:
